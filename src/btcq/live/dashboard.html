How these checks work
Live checks
Recomputed every 30 s from what the bot saves every minute: its heartbeat, its state, and a health record of its connections (Binance websocket and REST, Telegram, and in live mode the Binance account API). They need no network.
Full checks
Run when you press the button, and automatically every 15 minutes. The server calls Binance's and Telegram's public addresses and compares the bot's latest prices, F1's current 4h bar and D1's daily closes and positions with Binance's own data. No API key is used and nothing is changed.
Statuses
✓ OK: fine. ▲ Watch: unusual, look again soon. ✗ Problem: needs attention. … Waiting: not enough data yet or not checked yet. – Off: does not apply (for example the account API in paper mode).
What do these terms mean?
R
Profit or loss divided by the amount risked at the stop. With 1% risk per trade, +1 R ≈ +1% of the account.
Paper
Simulated trades on real Binance prices. Nothing is sent to Binance and nothing appears on binance.com.
Live account
Real orders on Binance (or the testnet). Visible on Binance under Futures → Positions and Open Orders.
Model
In live mode, the paper F1 book keeps running as the backtest's ideal, to compare real fills against.
Shadow
F1 long-only, paper only: it tests whether dropping short trades would have been better.
D1
Round 5's daily trend ensemble, paper only and information only: 5 coins × 9 signal lengths = 45 small long-only positions, each with 1/45 of its own $10,000 paper account, decided once a day at 00:00 UTC.
Drawdown
How far equity is below its highest point. New entries pause automatically at the kill-switch limit.
Stop
The price where a losing trade is closed automatically. In live mode it is an order resting on Binance.
